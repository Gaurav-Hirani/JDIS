# Final JDIS Risk Score Specification

## 1. Calibrated Probability Source
The raw probabilities are generated by the XGBoost Classifier and calibrated using an **Isotonic Regression** wrapper. This calibrator was exclusively fitted on the 2015 Validation set.

## 2. Risk Score Formula
The core risk score maps the continuous 0.0–1.0 calibrated probability to a deterministic integer scale:
```python
risk_score = floor(calibrated_probability * 100)
```

## 3. Interpretable Risk Bands
To aid operational triage, the continuous score is mapped to product-level bands. 

| Band | Score Range | Definition |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Low** | 0 - 20 | Very low baseline risk of exceeding 24 months. |
| **Moderate** | 21 - 50 | Standard risk cases below decision boundary. |
| **High** | 51 - 80 | Elevated risk of severe delay. |
| **Very High** | 81 - 100 | Near certainty of severe delay based on historical throughput. |

## 4. Band Distributions
**Validation Distribution (2015 - Used to fit Calibrator):**
- Low: 15,685 cases (31.38%) | Observed Delay: 10.14%
- Moderate: 16,977 cases (33.97%) | Observed Delay: 35.93%
- High: 12,648 cases (25.31%) | Observed Delay: 62.12%
- Very High: 4,670 cases (9.34%) | Observed Delay: 82.31%

**Test Distribution (2016 - Out-of-Sample Empirical Evaluation):**
- Low: 15,513 cases (31.07%) | Observed Delay: 9.76%
- Moderate: 19,975 cases (40.01%) | Observed Delay: 30.90%
- High: 10,603 cases (21.24%) | Observed Delay: 58.12%
- Very High: 3,838 cases (7.69%) | Observed Delay: 78.74%

## 5. Limitations
The risk bands represent an interpretable product categorization. Their usefulness is evaluated empirically, but they are not clinically, legalistically, or causally validated. They represent historical predictive associations available at the time of filing.
